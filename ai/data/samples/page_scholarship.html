Atlas Merit Scholarship 2027

Atlas Digital Foundation — announced 12 October 2026

The Atlas Merit Scholarship 2027 supports outstanding undergraduate and master's students in Algeria and across Africa who want to study computer science, data science or a related field. The scholarship covers full tuition for one academic year plus a monthly stipend of 40,000 DZD, and every scholar is paired with an industry mentor.

Eligibility

  • Open to students enrolled in an accredited university
  • Under 30 years old at the time of application
  • Minimum GPA of 14/20 or equivalent
  • French or English working proficiency

Benefits

  • Full tuition coverage for one academic year
  • Monthly stipend of 40,000 DZD
  • One-to-one industry mentorship

Applications open on 1 December 2026. The application deadline is 15 March 2027, 23:59 UTC. Late applications are not accepted. Selected candidates are notified by 30 April 2027.

Apply through the foundation's online portal; no application fee is charged.
