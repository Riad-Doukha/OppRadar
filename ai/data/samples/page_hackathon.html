Sahara Code Jam 2027

Dune Labs, remote-first hackathon

Sahara Code Jam 2027 is a 48-hour online hackathon where student teams build open-source tools for water, transport and education in the Sahara region. Teams of two to four people compete for a prize pool of 1,200,000 DZD plus six months of incubation support.

Who can apply

  • Students only — university or bootcamp enrolment required
  • Teams of 2 to 4 members, any nationality
  • All project code must be published under an MIT licence

What participants get

  • Prize pool of 1,200,000 DZD split across the top three teams
  • Six months of incubation support for winning teams
  • Mentorship sessions with Dune Labs engineers

Registration closes on 2027-04-10 at 18:00 GMT. The hackathon itself runs from 22 to 24 April 2027. Participation is fully remote and free.
